# Prompt delle illustrazioni

Le quattro immagini sono state generate con lo strumento integrato `image_gen`, mantenendo stile, palette e il filo narrativo color corallo coerenti. Tutte le immagini sono prive di testo incorporato: titoli e descrizioni restano in HTML per leggibilità, accessibilità e manutenzione.

## 01 — Fondamenta

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: responsive website editorial illustration, first stage of a four-image visual curriculum journey. Illustrate the first two years of an Italian upper-secondary Services for Health and Social Care course as the moment when students learn to observe the whole person, understand needs, discover welfare services and collect information responsibly. Bright flexible classroom opening visually toward a small stylized town map and community service network. A diverse group of 15–16-year-old students collaboratively studies a human-body model, a needs map, service cards and a laptop; a subtle continuous coral path begins in the foreground and leads onward. Sophisticated contemporary editorial illustration, tactile cut-paper shapes mixed with soft grain and clean geometric forms, warm and human-centered, educational but not childish. Wide 16:10 landscape, clear main action, generous breathing room, strong readable silhouettes. Deep indigo, warm cream, coral, teal and mustard accents. Show learning and observation rather than clinical treatment. Inclusive ages, skin tones and abilities; realistic respectful body language. No visible brands, text, letters, numbers, captions, logos, watermark or medical red-cross symbol. Avoid photorealism, stock-photo look, hospital drama, infantilizing imagery, clutter and pseudo-text.

## 02 — Relazione

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: responsive website editorial illustration, second stage of the same journey. Illustrate the third year, when students move from knowledge to professional relationship: listening, communicating across differences, working in a multidisciplinary team and recognizing the person’s needs. Welcoming community learning laboratory with an open circular table and subtle visual links toward local services. Several 16–17-year-old students practice an attentive interview and case discussion with a teacher-facilitator, an older adult and a parent with child represented respectfully; speech and listening are shown through gestures, cards and eye contact. The same coral path crosses the room and continues onward. Match the first image’s tactile cut-paper editorial style and palette. Wide 16:10 landscape, circular composition suggesting dialogue and teamwork. Warm midday light, empathy, active listening and cooperation. Student learning context, not a clinical consultation. Inclusive ages, cultures, skin tones and abilities; respectful non-paternalistic body language. No visible brands, text, letters, numbers, captions, logos, watermark or medical red-cross symbol. Avoid photorealism, stock-photo look, hospital scenes, savior imagery, infantilizing older or disabled people, clutter and pseudo-text.

## 03 — Progetto

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: responsive website editorial illustration, third stage of the same journey. Illustrate the fourth year, when students learn to co-design a personalized intervention, support autonomy, make environments safer and run inclusive educational activities. A bright accessible community apartment and activity space combined as one coherent learning lab. Students work with a multidisciplinary mentor and community participants around a large visual project plan; one evaluates an accessible room layout, another prepares an inclusive creative activity, another maps goals and resources with the person at the center of decisions. The same coral path links all actions and continues onward. Match the established tactile cut-paper editorial style and palette. Wide 16:10 landscape, person-centered project plan as focal point, three connected zones. Afternoon light, agency, practical problem-solving and shared responsibility. Emphasize co-design, autonomy and safety. Students do not perform invasive medical procedures. Disabled and older participants are active decision-makers. No visible brands, text, letters, numbers, captions, logos, watermark or medical red-cross symbol. Avoid photorealism, stock-photo look, hospital drama, savior imagery, infantilizing people, dominant clinical uniforms, clutter and pseudo-text.

## 04 — Profilo in uscita

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: responsive website editorial illustration, final stage of the same journey. Illustrate the fifth year and outgoing profile of an Italian Services for Health and Social Care graduate: coordinating people and territorial networks, supporting a personalized project, safeguarding rights, documenting outcomes and improving services with reliable data. An open community hub is visually connected to a home, school, social service, health service and public space; the coral path reaches a luminous central network and continues toward the horizon. A confident 18–19-year-old graduate, not portrayed as a doctor or nurse, facilitates a multidisciplinary meeting with the person and family at the center. Nearby are a secure abstract data dashboard, a project board without writing and community partners connected through colored threads. Match the established tactile cut-paper editorial style and palette. Wide 16:10 landscape, graduate as connector rather than hero. Golden late-afternoon light, competence, responsibility, possibility and belonging. Communicate co-design, organization, assistance, prevention, rights, secure information and evaluation without labels. No invasive care actions or claim that the graduate is a licensed health professional. Disabled and older people are shown with agency. No text, letters, numbers, captions, logos, watermark or medical red-cross symbol. Avoid photorealism, stock-photo look, graduation-cap clichés, hospital drama, savior imagery, hierarchy, clutter and pseudo-text.
